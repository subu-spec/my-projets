#Title: Designing the Future of Food Delivery: Crave Choice UI/UX Case Study.

I'm thrilled to share my latest project, Crave Choice! The goal was to revolutionise the user
experience by focusing on time-specific cravings.

The app features a streamlined sign-in process that immediately divides the user journey into
two distinct sections: Snacks and Food, catering to immediate needs, whether it's a mid-afternoon break or a full meal.
My research included a comprehensive competitor analysis of Swiggy and Zomato.

The analysis revealed that while they offer vast options, they lack specific focus on local
specialities. To resolve this, the interface features dedicated sections highlighting particular
shops known for specific items like coffee or tea.

For the design system, a strict colour palette of red, orange, and green was chosen because
these colours are known to stimulate appetite and excitement. Orange was specifically selected
for its vibrant energy and ability to capture the playful, craving-based intent of the
application.
